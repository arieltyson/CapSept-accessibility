# Known issues

Gaps we know about, and the plan to fix each one.

| Issue | Who it affects | Plan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Speaking tasks record the microphone, which also picks up VoiceOver speech when no headphones are connected. CapSept announces a reminder to use headphones before recording. | VoiceOver users without headphones | Pause VoiceOver output during recording when the system allows it. |
| Listening exercises cannot be answered by people who cannot hear the audio; the captions setting shows the transcript, which turns the exercise into reading practice. | Deaf and hard-of-hearing learners | This reflects the exam itself; the official tests offer accommodations through their test centres. |
| The paging scroll of the feed still slides with Reduce Motion on. The Next button moves without animation. | People sensitive to motion | Replace paging with a cross-fade when Reduce Motion is on. |
| Exam guidance (task instructions, strategy cards) is in English in both interface languages. | French-interface users | Translate exam guidance in a content update. |
